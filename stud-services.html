For Queens, Big and Small

Stud Services

Ralphy, our seal point Ragdoll, and Starsky, our blue silver Maine Coon, are both dual registered with the GCCF and TICA, and listed on the official GCCF Stud Register. Health tested and proven, they are available to carefully matched, GCCF-registered queens. In line with GCCF rules, we do not stud to unregistered queens. We take our responsibility to the breed seriously.

What's Included

Our stud service

  • A clean, quiet, dedicated stud area
  • Full GCCF health testing (HCM, PKD, and breed-appropriate screens)
  • Up-to-date vaccinations and regular parasite treatments
  • Experienced, hands-on supervision throughout the visit
  • A typical stay of three to four days, depending on activity
  • Daily updates by WhatsApp so you always know how your queen is settling
  • Friendly, honest advice on what to expect before and after
  • In the rare event of an unsuccessful mating, your queen is welcome back free of charge within eight weeks
Pricing: Ralphy, £300 per service. Starsky, £400 per service. Payment is made when your queen is dropped off. No deposit required.
Please note: all stud bookings are reviewed and approved individually. We'll always have a proper conversation about your queen, her health and her bloodlines before anything is confirmed.
