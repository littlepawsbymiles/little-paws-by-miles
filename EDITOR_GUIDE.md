# How to edit the Little Paws website

A quick guide for Mum & Mark. Nothing technical — if you can use Facebook, you can use this.

---

## Logging in

1. Go to **https://littlepawsbymiles.co.uk/admin**
2. Click **Log in with GitHub** (or use your email and password if you prefer)
3. You'll see the admin dashboard

---

## What you can do

The left-hand sidebar has five sections:

- **Cats** — edit descriptions, photos and registration for each queen and stud
- **Available kittens** — add or remove the kittens currently looking for homes
- **Upcoming litters** — tell visitors about litters you're expecting
- **Testimonials** — add reviews from happy kitten families
- **Site settings** — change your Instagram link, form URLs, etc.

---

## Adding a new available kitten

1. Click **Available kittens** in the sidebar
2. Click **New kitten** (top right)
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Display order:** 0, 1, 2, 3… (lowest number shows first on the page)
   - **Name:** e.g. "Biscuit"
   - **Breed:** pick from the dropdown
   - **Sex:** pick from the dropdown
   - **Colour:** e.g. "Blue Mitted"
   - **Price:** e.g. "£1,200" (include the £ sign)
   - **Status:** "Available" or "Reserved"
   - **Photo:** click in the box and drag a photo in, or click to browse
4. Click **Publish** (top right)
5. Wait about a minute, then check the site — your kitten is live

---

## Marking a kitten as sold or reserved

- **Reserved:** open the kitten, change **Status** to "Reserved", click Publish. The kitten stays on the page but shows as reserved.
- **Sold (gone to its new home):** open the kitten, click **Delete entry** at the bottom, confirm. The kitten is removed from the site.

---

## Adding photos to a cat's profile

1. Click **Cats** in the sidebar
2. Click the cat you want to edit (e.g. "Bella")
3. Scroll to the **Photos** section
4. Click **Add Photo**
5. Drag the image in, or click to browse your computer
6. You can add up to 5 photos per cat. **The first photo becomes the main image** on the card and at the top of the profile.
7. Click **Publish**

### Photo tips

- **Square photos** look best (the site crops to squares)
- **Good lighting** makes everything better — natural daylight by a window is ideal
- **Keep files under 2MB** — huge files slow the site down. If your phone takes big photos, run them through [Squoosh](https://squoosh.app) first (free, no signup, drag the file in and download a smaller version)

---

## Editing a cat's description

1. Click **Cats** in the sidebar
2. Click the cat to edit
3. Scroll to **About (full description)** — this is the rich text editor
4. Edit the text like you would in Word. Press Enter for new paragraphs. Use the **B** and **_I_** buttons for bold and italic.
5. Click **Publish**

---

## Updating an upcoming litter

1. Click **Upcoming litters** in the sidebar
2. Edit an existing one, or click **New litter**
3. Fill in breed, expected date (can be free text like "Early June 2026"), expected count, and any notes
4. When the litter arrives, delete this entry — then add the kittens themselves under **Available kittens**

---

## Adding a testimonial

1. Click **Testimonials** in the sidebar → **New testimonial**
2. Fill in: their first name + last initial (e.g. "Sarah M."), breed of their kitten, rating (usually 5), and their comment
3. Publish

---

## Connecting a Tally form (one-time setup)

The site has four forms (kitten enquiry, waitlist, stud booking, contact). Right now they're placeholders. Once you've made real forms on [tally.so](https://tally.so):

1. In Tally, open your form → **Share** → **Embed on your site** → copy the URL
2. In the CMS, go to **Site settings → Form embed URLs**
3. Paste the URL into the matching field (e.g. Waitlist form URL)
4. Publish

You only do this once per form. From then on, people can submit it directly on the site.

---

## A few important things

### "Publish" means live to the world
Anything you publish is visible to everyone immediately (well, after a 60-second build). There's no staging or draft. Check it looks right before publishing.

### You can undo anything
Every change is saved in version history. If you publish something and regret it, just edit it back. (If something goes badly wrong, ask Miles to "revert to the previous version" — he can do that in 30 seconds.)

### Don't change the "ID (URL slug)" for existing cats
This is the only scary warning. The ID is part of the cat's web address — if you change Bella's ID from `bella` to `bella-rose`, anyone with Bella's URL bookmarked will get a broken link. For new cats you're adding, pick the ID once and leave it.

### Don't worry about the rest
If a field says "required" and you can't save — fill it in. Otherwise: explore, click around, try things. The worst you can do is publish something silly, and you can always edit it back.
