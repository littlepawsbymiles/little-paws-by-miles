Mark and I have been raising cats at home for a while now, and after a lot of nudging from friends (and from Oli, who built the site), we're finally starting to write things down.

This little blog is where we'll share the everyday side of what we do. Kitten updates as litters grow. Notes on breed care that we've picked up the hard way. The occasional bit of news, and probably a fair few photos of cats sleeping in places cats shouldn't sleep.

We won't post to a schedule. When there's something worth saying we'll say it, and when there isn't we won't. That said, if you have a question about our cats or our kittens that you think other people might have too, drop us a line — we'll happily write an answer up here.

Thanks for stopping by.

Julia
