We're Julia and Mark, and Little Paws by Miles is simply our house with cats in it. A lot of cats, admittedly, but they've always been family first and breeding has always come second. That's the bit we'd like you to understand before anything else.

The cats live with us properly. They're on the sofa, under the bed, following us into the kitchen when they hear the fridge. Our kittens grow up in the middle of all of that, which is why they tend to arrive in their new homes already quite sure about people.

We've added to our little family slowly over the years. The only cats we keep are ones we'd happily live with forever, whether or not they ever go on to have kittens. That's the standard, and it isn't one we bend on.
