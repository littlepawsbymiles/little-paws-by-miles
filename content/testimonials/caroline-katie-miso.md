Julia is a wonderful breeder and made our experience of bringing home Miso super special. She was warm and welcoming, letting us into her home to meet her cats and explained her journey as a breeder. She gave us time to sit with the kittens alone, and meet their beautiful mum, Bella. She also took the time to tell us everything we needed to know about Ragdolls as first-time owners.

She kept us updated with how Miso was getting on as he grew and shared regular updates with us until he was ready to come home.

Because Miso had been well socialised he settled really well when we brought him home, and he has never been scared of any of our visitors! Everyone who meets him adores him!

Miso is an absolutely amazing kitten and cat, everything we had looked for in a cat, he has the sweetest personality. We couldn't have asked for a better breeder and would highly recommend Julia.
