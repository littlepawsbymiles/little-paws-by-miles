We came to view kittens and fell in love with our baby Cleo at five weeks old. My husband and I were blown away by how incredible Julia and Mark's set up is for their cats and how sociable, loving and confident their cats are. They are truly part of their family, which is exactly what we were looking for.

In the weeks counting down until we could collect Cleo, their communication, care and picture and video updates were second to none. We felt so confident every step of the way, and when we finally brought our baby Cleo home, she was full of confidence, curiosity and affection.

She is so sociable, so sweet and just such fun. She was immediately happy with other cats, children and dogs, content to be groomed and vocal about wanting cuddles. Her incredible temperament is a testament to Julia and Mark and how well they raised her. We can't wait for future litters to bring Cleo home a new playmate.
