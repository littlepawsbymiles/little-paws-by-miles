Ralphy is our Seal Point Ragdoll boy. Large, chunky, with rich seal-brown points on a soft creamy coat, deep sapphire eyes, a plush full ruff, breeches, and a proper plume of a tail. He looks every bit the Ragdoll and has the teddy-bear personality to match.

He carries chocolate and dilute, which makes him a genuinely useful boy to work with. And he's proven, many times over, with excellent fertility and consistently healthy litters averaging six to seven kittens. His most recent litter was eight.

Available to healthy queens.

## Temperament

Ralphy is calm, gentle and very people-focused. He's wonderfully relaxed with visiting queens, which makes him a good fit for experienced breeders and first-time stud users alike. He's also one of the most affectionate cats in the house, which isn't always a given with a working boy.
