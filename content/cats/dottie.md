Dottie is Cookie's sister, and where Cookie is gentle, Dottie is properly affectionate. Stroke her coat and it feels like velvet. Ask her for a cuddle and she'll roll straight onto her back for a tummy rub without a second thought.

Her eyes are the first thing people notice. Deep, soulful blue, the kind that give her a slightly wistful look even when she's perfectly happy. Like Cookie, she isn't registered and isn't part of our breeding programme. She's a much-loved family cat, kept in the same warm household as the rest of our cats.

She brings a lovely calm to the house. Very easy to be around.
