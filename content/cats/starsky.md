Starsky is a young male of real Maine Coon type. Large, substantial, and built the way the breed ought to be. Broad chest, strong bone, square muzzle, high-set ears with proper lynx tips, and deep emerald eyes that give him a wild-but-gentle look.

His coat is the thing that tends to stop people in their tracks. Bold blue silver mackerel tabby markings, plush texture, full ear furnishings, and a flowing tail that wouldn't be out of place in a show ring. He has presence.

## Pedigree

Starsky comes from an internationally acclaimed pedigree featuring multiple titled cats and elite distinctions, including Grand International Champion (GIC), Supreme Grand Champion (SGC), International Winner (IW), and Quadruple Grand Champion (QGC) lines.

Key lines include Northern Wind Slav Story Ua, Honey Devil Passion Fruit, Caprice Consort, Avalan R Jack, Atlas Vom Dorenwald, Boston Artimax Elbarko and Clever Akella Leader.

Champion and title lines include GIC Alliance Zander, SGC Alliance Apollo, and IW/QGC lines including Heidi Ho Son Shine, SilentPartner The Matrix, Zephyr of Treasured Posey and Carissima Cowley's Lucky.

These lines consistently produce outstanding size, powerful structure, luxurious coat quality and proven show excellence. A five-generation pedigree is available on request.
