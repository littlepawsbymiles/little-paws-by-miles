Dottie is expecting her next litter, paired with Ralphy. We couldn't be more excited for her.

Dottie is one of the most affectionate girls in the house, the cuddle queen of the Ragdoll side, and she has that velvet coat and those deep blue eyes we love about the blue points. Like her sister Cookie, she isn't registered, but she is fully health checked and raised to exactly the same standard as our registered queens.

Ralphy is our own GCCF and TICA registered seal point Ragdoll stud, listed on the official GCCF Stud Register. Pairing him with Dottie should bring out the best of both. Get in touch and we'll happily talk through the registration position for individual kittens.

Colours, sex and availability will all be confirmed once the kittens arrive and have had a proper vet check. We never guess at these things and we don't take deposits against kittens that don't yet exist.

What we can say is that every kitten from this litter will leave us at thirteen weeks old. Vet-checked, vaccinated, microchipped, fully socialised, and with a full kitten information pack covering everything you'll need for the first few weeks at home.

## Want to hear more?

If you'd like to be considered for one of Dottie's kittens, please do get in touch. We keep a small, informal waitlist rather than a ticketed queue, and we always have a proper conversation before anything is decided. The right home matters as much to us as the right kitten.
