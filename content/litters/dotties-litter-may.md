On the 7th of May, in the very early hours of the morning, Dottie welcomed seven beautiful kittens into the world.

Well... she welcomed the first two down the side of the sofa unbeknown to me. It just goes to show you really cannot force a queen to have her kittens where you want them to. Dottie clearly had her own ideas for her first-time mum experience.

Thankfully, I quickly rescued the first two babies and settled everyone safely into the birthing bed. After that, Dottie relaxed beautifully and went on to deliver the remaining kittens calmly and confidently. She has been an absolutely wonderful mummy ever since.

It is far too early to say much about colours, weights or markings. The first week is all about Dottie and her babies, getting them warm, fed, and settled. We will know more in a fortnight or so once their points start to come through.

## About this pairing

Dottie is our blue point Ragdoll girl, the cuddle queen of the household. Like her sister Cookie, she isn't registered, but she's been raised to exactly the same standard as our registered queens, with full health checks throughout.
